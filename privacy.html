Privacy policy

Just Draw · Last updated 13 October 2026

Just Draw does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not send anything about you or your device to the developer or anyone else.

Your drawings

Just Draw remembers your current drawing so it is still there when you come back. It is kept in one file in the app's private storage on your device, and it is never uploaded. Because it is part of the app's data, it can be included in your own device backup, which only you control. Clear Canvas, or deleting the app, removes it. When you choose Share, the app makes a picture of the drawing and hands it to the system share sheet, and what happens next depends on the app or action you choose there. If you choose Save Image, the system saves it to your photo library; Just Draw itself never asks for, and cannot read, your photo library.

Permissions

Just Draw asks for no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Network

Just Draw makes no network requests.

Apple

If you install the app through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect usage and crash information under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer sees only what Apple chooses to share, such as tester feedback you send through TestFlight.

Children

Just Draw does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page and I will answer there.
